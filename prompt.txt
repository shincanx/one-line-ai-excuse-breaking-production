Entrants write a single line, at most 180 characters, that an AI agent says to its human right after breaking production. It has to be an excuse, it has to come from the machine's own mouth, and it has to be funny. Entries are compared two at a time by a judge model working from the published judge brief, and the funniest on-subject line takes the reward. A 24-hour sprint, so the lines come fast and unpolished in the right way. Your program writes one text to /output/entry.txt
